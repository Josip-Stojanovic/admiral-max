The name over the door

Maksimilijan Njegovan

1858 to 1930

Admiral of the Austro-Hungarian Navy, Croatian by birth, commander of the fleet during the First World War.

A konoba that remembers the sailors of the old fleet

For a century and a half the Istrian coast sent its men to sea under the Habsburg naval ensign. Admiral Max takes its name from the highest-ranking of them, Maksimilijan Njegovan, and is kept in memory of every Austro-Hungarian sailor and the families who waited for them in the harbour towns.

The room reflects it. Brass, dark wood, charts and ship models were assembled together with Gallerion, Novigrad's museum of the Austro-Hungarian navy, a few streets away in the old town. Outside, the terrace sits at the foot of the Porporela breakwater, with the boats of the Mandrač on one side and the open Adriatic on the other.

The cooking is simpler than the story. Fish and shellfish come from the local boats and are grilled, fried or cooked in buzara the way Novigrad has always done it. Bread is baked in house and arrives with Istrian olive oil. Portions are generous and the bill is honest.

What guests come back for

  • The fish platter for two

    The most mentioned dish by a distance. Whole grilled fish, scampi, squid and a ring of scallops on one board, with chard and potatoes. Several regulars call it the best on the coast.

  • Small Novigrad scallops

    The bay's specialty, where river water meets the sea. Guests single out the small ones, grilled with olive oil and garlic.

  • Portions and the bill

    People leave full and surprised by the total. Value for money is the second most common remark after the fish.

  • The terrace

    Dinner with the boats coming in. Guests recommend reserving a terrace table in season.
